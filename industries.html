Timeline 2026–2050

Twenty-four years in five eras.

One plausible path from today to 2050. The dates are imagined. The order is the useful part: each era depends on the one before it.

Speculative scenario · not a forecast
2026–2030Agents arrive
  • 2027AI assistants start completing multi-step tasks, not just answering questions.
  • 2028The first rules on AI agents acting for consumers take effect.
  • 2030Most knowledge workers delegate routine work to agents daily.
2030–2035Science speeds up
  • 2031Self-driving labs become standard in materials and drug research.
  • 2033An AI-designed drug is approved for a common disease.
  • 2035Content provenance becomes the default on major platforms.
2035–2040Machines get bodies
  • 2036General-purpose robots become affordable for small businesses.
  • 2038Home robots reach mass-market prices.
  • 2040Several countries trial shorter standard work weeks.
2040–2045Rules catch up
  • 2041International treaty on testing the most capable AI systems.
  • 2043Fiduciary duties for personal agents become law in many regions.
  • 2045The first commercial fusion plants connect to the grid.
2045–2050Intelligence as infrastructure
  • 2047Continuous preventive health monitoring is covered by public health systems.
  • 2049Most electricity comes from zero-carbon sources.
  • 2050AI is ordinary: present everywhere, noticed rarely, governed in public.
