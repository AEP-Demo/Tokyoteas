About the project

A field guide to a future that hasn't happened yet.

Axiom 2050 is a speculative publication. We take trends visible today, follow them a generation forward and describe what daily life might feel like. Nothing here is a forecast. It's a way of thinking about choices while they're still choices.

Mission & vision

Why this project exists.

Mission
To imagine the age of advanced AI clearly enough that people can choose it, shape it, or refuse parts of it.
Vision
A 2050 where intelligence is abundant, its benefits are widely shared, and the most important decisions stay in human hands.
How we build a scenario

From a signal today to a scene in 2050.

Every dispatch follows the same four steps, so readers can see where the imagination starts.

StepWhat we do
01 · SignalFind a real trend, product or research result from today.
02 · StretchFollow it forward 24 years at a plausible pace.
03 · CollideCombine it with other trends to see what changes together.
04 · GroundDescribe it through one ordinary person's day.
Principles

What we hold ourselves to.

Plausible, not certain

Every scenario is labeled as imagined. We never present a guess as a fact.

Hopeful, not naive

We describe a good future and the risks that could prevent it.

Human-scale

Big changes are told through ordinary lives, because that's how they're felt.

Open to argument

Readers' predictions shape what we write next.
