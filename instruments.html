Accelerators

Ready-made tools that speed up every engagement.

Our accelerators are repeatable, productized frameworks. Each one measures a specific part of your data practice and produces a result you can act on.

Audit

Tag Health Scan

Every tag, variable and event on your key journeys, checked against your design.

Output
Tag inventory, defect list by severity, fix plan
Platforms
Adobe Tags, Web SDK, GTM, GA4
Architecture

Identity Stitch Blueprint

A first-party identity design using FPID and ECID, joined to CRM and app IDs.

Output
Identity namespace map, cookie plan, implementation spec
Platforms
Web SDK, AEP Identity Service, GA4
Design

SDR Builder

A solution design reference that ties every collected field to a KPI and an owner.

Output
SDR workbook, data layer spec, XDM mapping
Platforms
Adobe Analytics, CJA, GA4

KPI Tree

Business goals broken down into measurable drivers your teams can move.

Output
