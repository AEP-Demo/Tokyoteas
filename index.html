The name

Axio + metriq: two axes, one measure of value.

Axio refers to the two axes we plot every engagement on. Metriq is measurement, with one difference. The q, underlined in our logo, stands for quality: the value of a measurement, not just its count.

The Axis Method

How an engagement runs.

  1. Assess

    Audit what you collect, how it's used and where trust breaks.

  2. Architect

    Design the KPI tree, data layer, schemas and identity strategy.

  3. Implement

    Build and QA tags, SDKs, connectors and dashboards.

  4. Activate

    Put the data to work in audiences, tests and journeys.

  5. Optimize

    Measure the outcome, coach your team, refine the roadmap.
