Breakthroughs

When discovery runs at machine speed.

AI doesn't replace scientists. It gives each of them a tireless lab partner that reads everything, tests thousands of ideas overnight and admits when it's wrong. These are six discoveries that partnership could speed up.

How it works

The self-driving lab, step by step.

The loop that makes these breakthroughs fast. Each turn of it takes hours instead of months.

StepWho does itWhat happens
01ScientistSets the goal and the limits: what to find, what's safe, what it may cost.
02AI modelReads the literature and proposes thousands of candidate ideas.
03SimulationScreens the candidates and keeps the most promising few hundred.
04Robotic labRuns real experiments around the clock and records every result.
05AI modelLearns from the results and proposes the next round.
06ScientistChecks the surprising results, decides what to publish and what to stop.
