03 / How to choose

Start with the
question.

The right instrument follows from what you need to know, the conditions you measure in, and how the data will be used.

Step 01

Define the measurand

What exactly must be measured, and to what tolerance or detection limit.

Step 02

Map the environment

Temperature, vibration, contamination, access and the pace of the process.

Step 03

Plan the data

Sampling rate, traceability, integration with your systems and reporting.

Step 04

Validate on site

Trial measurements on your own samples before committing to a configuration.
