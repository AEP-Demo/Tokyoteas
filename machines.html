Machines

The stack behind the world of 2050.

Six layers, from the power plant to the interface. Each layer depends on the one beneath it, which is why cheap clean energy is the foundation of everything else.

Speculative scenario · not a forecast
From the ground up

Six layers, bottom to top.

Layer1
Energy & compute

Intelligence runs on power.

Chips that compute with light and brain-inspired circuits use a fraction of today's energy. Data centers sit beside solar farms, wind and the first fusion plants.

Photonic and neuromorphic chips · energy-aware scheduling

Layer2
World models

Models that understand how things work.

Beyond predicting text, models learn physics, chemistry and cause and effect from video, sensors and experiments. They keep learning after deployment, within limits.

Multimodal world models · continual learning · verified reasoning

Layer3
Personal agents

An assistant with a legal duty to you.

Your agent acts on your behalf like a trusted professional: it can't be bought by advertisers, and it must explain its decisions on request.

Fiduciary agents · on-device memory · personal data vaults

Layer4
Embodied machines

Robots that learn a new task in an afternoon.

General-purpose robots learn from demonstration and simulation. They're shaped for their work: arms on rails, wheeled carriers, crawlers and some humanoids.

Robot foundation models · shared skill libraries · safety envelopes

Layer5
Interfaces

Computing without a screen to stare at.

Voice, light glasses and gestures replace most screens. Non-invasive neural input helps people with paralysis and speech loss communicate.

Ambient voice · AR glasses · medical neural interfaces

Layer6
Trust & provenance

Every claim and image can be checked.

Cryptographic signatures show where media came from and whether it was altered. Agents verify each other before they transact.

Content credentials · agent identity · audit trails
