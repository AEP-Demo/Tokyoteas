About Axiometriq

Measurement with a point of view.

Axiometriq is a data analytics consultancy built on a simple idea: a measurement is only worth what it helps you decide. We plot our work on two axes and hold ourselves to the point where both are high.

The name

Why the q is underlined.

Our name has three parts. Each one is a commitment.

Axio

Two axes

From the Greek axon, the axis. We measure every engagement on two: customer satisfaction and growth mindset.

Metri

Measurement

From metron, a measure. Our craft is instrumenting digital experiences so numbers can be trusted.

q̲

Quality, made visible

The q replaces the c of metric. The underscore marks that difference: we measure value, not volume.

What most teams have
metric

A count. Page views, sessions, clicks. Easy to collect, easy to misread, rarely tied to a decision.

What we deliver
metriq

A measure with a defined owner, a definition everyone agrees on, verified collection and a decision it informs.

Mission & vision

Why we exist, and where we're headed.

Mission
To turn customer data into measurable value by delivering analytics of the highest quality, built by people who never stop growing.
Vision
A world where every business decision is plotted on two axes, the satisfaction of the customer and the growth of the people serving them, and measured by the value it creates.
The two axes

Where both axes are high, value appears.

A team that delivers well but stops learning falls behind the platforms. A team that learns but delivers poorly loses the client's trust. We need both, and we plot ourselves honestly.

X axis · Customer satisfaction

Delivered at the highest quality

Every tag, data layer, dashboard and audience is tested, documented and handed over so it keeps working after we leave. Clients judge us on accuracy and adoption, and so do we.

Y axis · Growth mindset

Shared by employees and employer

Our consultants grow through certification, mentoring and real problems. Our clients' teams grow through enablement built into every engagement. A company only rises as fast as its people.

Values

How we work with each other and with clients.

Precision

We define a metric before we collect it, and we verify it before anyone reports on it.

Ownership

If we built it, we document it, monitor it and fix it. No orphaned tags.

Curiosity

The platforms change every quarter. So does what we know.

Respect for data

Consent, privacy and governance are designed in from the first schema, not bolted on.

Candor

We say when a number is wrong, a test is underpowered or a tool isn't needed.

Shared growth

We count an engagement a success when your team can run it without us.
