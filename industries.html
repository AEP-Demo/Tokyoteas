Industries

Different questions, the same discipline.

Each industry measures success differently. The method stays the same: define the decision, instrument it properly, join it to one identity and act on it.

Retail & ecommerce

Product discovery, cart abandonment, loyalty and omnichannel attribution.

Typical KPIs
Conversion rate, AOV, repeat purchase rate

Financial services

Application funnels, authenticated journeys and strict consent requirements.

Typical KPIs
Application completion, cost per account, digital adoption

Travel & hospitality

Search-to-book funnels, loyalty profiles and dynamic offers.

Typical KPIs
Look-to-book ratio, direct booking share, ancillary revenue

Media & entertainment

Content engagement, subscriptions and churn across web, app and connected TV.

Typical KPIs
Engaged time, subscriber retention, churn rate

Healthcare & life sciences

Patient and HCP journeys with careful handling of sensitive data.

Typical KPIs
Appointment completion, portal adoption, content reach

B2B & SaaS

Account-based journeys, trials, product usage and pipeline attribution.

Typical KPIs
Trial-to-paid rate, pipeline influenced, product activation
